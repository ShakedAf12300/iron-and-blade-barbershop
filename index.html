Our Story

Where old‑school craft meets modern edge

Iron & Blade was born in 2012 from a simple belief: a great haircut is more than a service — it's a ritual. We pair time‑honoured techniques like the hot‑towel straight razor shave with contemporary fades and precision styling.

Pull up a leather chair, grab a coffee or a whisky, and leave the rest to us. No rush, no shortcuts — just meticulous detail and a look that's unmistakably yours.

  • Master barbers with 10+ years experience
  • Premium, locally crafted grooming products
  • Complimentary drink with every service
